I believe the best design gets out of the way. My job is to make things so intuitive that nobody notices the design at all.

Greta — UX Designer

Hey, I'm Greta.

I've been designing digital products for over five years, with a focus on the intersection of human behaviour and technology. My work spans fintech, healthcare, and e-commerce — industries where good UX isn't a "nice to have," it's critical.

My process always starts with understanding people. Before I touch a wireframe, I want to know who we're designing for, what frustrates them, and what they're ultimately trying to achieve. Research isn't the boring part — it's where all the interesting ideas live.

Outside of work you'll find me haunting independent coffee shops, taking photographs on film, and arguing (constructively) about whether serif or sans-serif is better for body copy. The answer is Inter. Always Inter.
